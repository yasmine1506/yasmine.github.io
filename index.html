About

I am currently pursuing my M.S. Degree in Computer Science and Applications at Virginia Tech.
I am a member of the Crowd Intelligence Lab where I work with my advisor, Dr. Kurt Luther.
Within computer science, my research interests lie in Human Computer Interation, social-human-centered design, social computing, crowdsourcing and the interweaving of the arts in technology.
I have received my Bachelor's Degree Magna Cum Laude in Computer Science with a minor in Industrial Design and French from Virginia Tech in May 2019.

CrowdTrace: Visualizing provenance in distributed sensemaking

Team: Tianyi Li, Yasmine Belghith, Dr. Chris North, Dr. Kurt Luther

In this project, we explore provenance analysis for distributed sensemaking in the context of crowdsourcing, where distributed analysis contributions are captured in microtasks. We propose crowd auditing as a way to help individual analysts visualize and trace provenance to debug distributed sensemaking.

Tooling Bamboo Growth

Advisor: Prof. Jonas Hauptman

As part of an undergraduate independent study, my responsibilities were:

  • Design system wireframes for CNC machine tailored to Bamboo. Schematics include 4-axes in addition to a mirrored y-axis and supported by LinuxCNC (MachineKit) and BeagleBone boards.
  • Research user interface alternatives for MachineKit to enable customization of controls for the Bamboo CNC machine.

PPP: Parallel Programming with Pictures

Advisor: Dr. Wu Feng

As part of an undergraduate independent study, my responsibilities were:

  • Assemble generalized modules to teach “parallel programming with pictures” for relevant pedagogical venues, e.g., CS 2104, Let’s Code Blacksburg, and so on. Modules engage students to work with serial and parallel computing concepts and interact with the block-based language “Snap!”
  • Participate in Women in Computing Day by assisting, tutoring, and walking through the “parallel programming with pictures” modules with the attendees.
